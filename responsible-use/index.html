About ScoreMind analysis

Understand the statistics, model estimates and AI explanations you see in ScoreMind.

Updated November 2, 2026

Model estimates

Probabilities describe possible match outcomes. They are informative estimates, not guarantees. Historical results do not ensure future outcomes.

Sports information and analysis

ScoreMind brings together scores, match statistics, team comparisons and AI-assisted explanations. Optional subscriptions and digital purchases provide access to additional app features.

Read the full context

Consider recent form, the amount of available data and the match context. A small sample gives a limited view. AI explanations can contain errors; check important details against the source data.

Data, screenshots and independence

Coverage varies by competition and match. Data can be delayed or incomplete. Screenshots illustrate the app at the time of capture. Team names and crests identify the teams shown and do not imply sponsorship or endorsement.
