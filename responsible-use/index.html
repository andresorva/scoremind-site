Responsible use & important information

Understand what ScoreMind offers, what its estimates mean and where to find support.

Updated 20 October 2026

18+ on this website

This website is intended for adults aged 18 or over, or the higher minimum age required locally.

Estimates, not guarantees

Predictions, probabilities, daily selections and ScoreBot responses are for information and entertainment. They do not guarantee match results, profits or financial returns. Historical results do not ensure future outcomes. AI responses can contain errors.

What the service does

ScoreMind supplies sports information and statistical analysis. It does not accept bets, hold betting funds or pay winnings. Optional subscriptions and digital purchases pay for content or app features; they are not stakes in a sporting outcome.

Keep control of your decisions

Do not rely on a prediction as a promise of income or a way to recover losses. If you choose to gamble elsewhere, follow local law, set limits and avoid chasing losses. Stop and seek support if it affects your wellbeing, relationships or finances.

Independent support

Gambling Therapy offers international online support in multiple languages. It is an external service, independent of ScoreMind. Availability and support options are determined by that provider.
