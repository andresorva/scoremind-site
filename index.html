GOOD TO KNOW

Before you download.

What can I do for free?+

Download the app, explore fixtures and results, and view the available daily selection. The app shows which features require Premium.

What does Premium add?+

Premium adds advanced match analysis and ScoreBot features. Review the current benefits, daily message allowance and any additional balance in the app before purchasing.

What do the probabilities mean?+

They are estimates from the model for a stated match outcome. They are not guarantees or verified historical success rates.

Do model estimates guarantee results?+

No. Sport is uncertain. Model estimates and AI commentary may be wrong; available data can also be incomplete or delayed.

Which matches are available?+

Browse the current fixture list in the app. Competitions, statistics, and analysis vary with data coverage. Check the current options inside ScoreMind.

Where can I download ScoreMind?+

Use the Google Play buttons on this page to open ScoreMind for Android. Availability is shown by Google Play for your device and region.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?+

Manage your subscription through the platform where you purchased it. For Google Play purchases, use Payments & subscriptions in Google Play. Check your plan’s renewal conditions there.

How can I delete my account?+

Open the account deletion page and follow its verification steps. Deleting an account is separate from managing a subscription; check the platform where you purchased it.
